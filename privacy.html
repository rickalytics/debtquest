Privacy policy

Progress feels better when you know who can see it.

Last updated September 13, 2026

Your private tracker belongs to your account. Joining a circle does not give other members access to your debt balances or notes.

You choose the wins you share. Payment amounts stay out of circle posts unless you opt in.

No advertising or cross-app tracking. DebtQuest does not sell your personal information.

This policy covers the DebtQuest iPhone app and website, maintained by Rick Griffith ("DebtQuest," "we," or "us"). It explains the information we process, why we use it, and how you can manage it. Available features depend on whether you use a cloud account, a device-only version, or the sample journey.

1. Information we use

You provide most of the information in DebtQuest directly. We use it to run the features you choose:

Account and profile
Your email, account identifier, display name, profile preferences, and authentication information let you sign in and use your account. Passwords are processed by our authentication provider for sign-in, password changes, and account-deletion verification.
Private financial records
Debt names and types, balances, interest rates, minimum payments, payment amounts and dates, and notes power your tracker and payoff estimates. DebtQuest does not connect to banks, move money, or request bank passwords or full payment-card numbers.
Goals and progress
Your reason for paying off debt, check-ins, points, levels, milestones, reward plans, and redemptions power your personal journey. Points and rewards have no monetary value.
Circle activity
Your display name, memberships, invitations, shared check-ins and payment wins, reactions, and their timestamps let you connect with your chosen people.
Support and safety
If you contact us, we receive your email, message, and anything you choose to attach. In-app reports store the reporter, reported member or activity, category, time, and review status. We also store blocks and moderation status to handle concerns and limit abuse.
Connection information
Our hosting and account providers process technical information to deliver and protect the service. This may include IP addresses, browser or device information, request times, and error or security logs.

2. Where your records live

Cloud accounts: Supabase provides authentication and stores your tracker and circle data so they are available when you sign in on another device. Your device stores session information to keep you signed in. Cloud changes require an internet connection.

Device-only versions: Tracker records are stored on that device. They are not automatically uploaded when you later sign in to a cloud account. Moving those records requires an explicit backup export and import. Visiting the website still involves our web host processing the request.

Sample journey: The demo uses fictional records in separate local storage. Sample actions do not change your real tracker or post to real circles. Avoid entering real financial details into a shared demo device.

DebtQuest uses local storage for app preferences, sessions, and sample data, and caches app files for the web app. It does not use advertising cookies or include analytics or advertising SDKs. Your operating system or browser may separately back up device data under your own settings.

3. What your circle sees

A circle is a shared activity space. Each member keeps their own private tracker.

Visibility to other circle members
Information When it is visible
Display name and membership When you create or join a circle; joining also creates a shared event.
Check-ins and payment wins When you choose to share them. Payment wins include your overall payoff percentage.
Payment amount Only when you enable "Include payment amounts" and share that payment.
Reactions When you give a cheer or heart; your name is shown with the reaction.
Debt names, balances, APRs, private notes, and reward plans Not included in circle posts or made available through your membership.

Sharing preferences apply to future posts. To remove earlier disclosures, use Settings → Remove my shared activity for each circle. Changing or removing a payment in your private tracker does not remove its existing circle post.

Anyone with an active invitation can join if there is space, subject to account and blocking restrictions. Invitations expire after seven days. Share links privately and revoke them in Settings if needed. Other members can keep screenshots or copies of what they have already seen; deleting a post cannot erase those copies.

4. Reports and blocking

In-app reports are available to authorized operators for investigation, not to other circle members. Blocking hides names, posts, and reactions between you and the blocked member in the app. It does not remove either account from the circle or delete previously shared information.

We use report details, relevant circle activity, and account information to investigate concerns and take action, such as removing an activity or pausing social access. Circle names and reactions use preset options; there is no free-form group chat or photo upload.

5. Service providers and disclosure

Supabase processes cloud account, tracker, and circle data. Vercel hosts the website and its public pages. Email providers deliver account emails and support correspondence. These services process information needed to authenticate users, store records, deliver requests, handle support, and protect the service.

Authorized operators may access cloud information when necessary for these purposes. We require providers handling personal information on our behalf to protect it consistently with this policy and use it only to provide the relevant services. Processing may occur outside the country where you live.

We do not sell personal information, share it with data brokers, or use it for targeted advertising or cross-app tracking. We may disclose limited information where legally required or necessary to address fraud, security incidents, or threats to people's safety.

6. Retention and deletion

Your cloud tracker remains associated with your account until you change or reset it or delete the account. There is no automatic expiration for inactive trackers or older circle posts. The feed shows recent activity; older stored posts are not automatically deleted when they leave that view.

  • Reset private tracker clears private debts, payments, progress, and personal rewards. Your login and circle activity remain.
  • Remove my shared activity removes your posts in that circle, reactions attached to those posts, and your reactions to other posts there.
  • Leave circle removes your membership, shared activity, and invitations there. If you own it, ownership passes to another member; an empty circle is removed.
  • Delete cloud account removes your login, tracker, social profile, memberships, posts, reactions, invitations, blocks, and reports you submitted from the active database. Circles you still own are removed for all members. Leave those circles first if you want someone else to keep them.

Reports submitted by other people may remain for safety review, with references to your deleted account or activity removed. Their reporters' identifiers remain until those reports or accounts are removed. Support correspondence is handled separately from your app account; contact us to request its deletion.

We retain support and safety records while needed to resolve a request, investigate abuse, protect the service, or meet legal obligations, and remove information no longer needed for those purposes. Provider backups and security or operational logs may retain copies after deletion until they expire under the relevant provider's retention settings. Deletion from active records does not immediately erase every backup or log.

Signing out or uninstalling the app does not delete your cloud account. Device-only records can be lost when you clear storage or remove the app. Downloaded backups, device backups, and copies shared with another service remain under the control of you or the destination you chose.

7. Your privacy choices

Edit your profile in Settings and your debt records in Journey. Use Settings → Export backup for a copy of your private tracker. This export does not include circle memberships, shared posts, or support records; contact us for help accessing other information associated with your account.

Choose whether to share a win and whether to include its amount. You can remove shared activity, revoke invitations, block members, or leave a circle in Settings. Use Settings → Delete cloud account to request deletion in the app; it requires your current password and the confirmation word DELETE. See the account-deletion guide if you need help.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your information, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent where applicable, or contact a privacy regulator. Contact us to make a request. We may need to verify that the request relates to your account; do not email passwords or sign-in links.

8. Keeping your information safe

Cloud connections use HTTPS. Account and circle access checks limit what other users can retrieve. Keep your device locked, protect your account password, and share invitation links only with people you trust.

Exported backups contain readable financial records and are not password-protected by DebtQuest. Store them somewhere private. If you contact support, hide balances and other people's details in screenshots and send only what is necessary to explain the issue.

9. Children

DebtQuest is designed for people managing their own finances and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it as appropriate.

10. Policy updates

We will update the date on this page when the policy changes. For material changes to how personal information is used, we will provide an appropriate notice and obtain permission where required.
