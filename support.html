Need a hand?

Contact support

Tell us what happened, what you expected, and the steps that led to the problem. Include your app version, iPhone/iOS version or browser, and any error message. For an account problem, contact us from the email you use to sign in when possible.

Your email app opens a draft; you choose what to send. Hide private details in screenshots. Never send passwords, bank credentials, sign-in links, or a full financial backup.

Accounts & sign-in

How do I start?

Enter the name you want to use and what you're making room for. Add a debt from Today or Journey, then log payments you've already made. Daily check-ins help you see your progress beyond the balance.

Each person uses their own account and private tracker. Join a circle to share selected wins with your partner or friends. Circles need a connected account; the sample journey uses fictional people.

I forgot my password or didn't get an email.

On the sign-in screen, choose Forgot your password? Enter your account email, then check your inbox and junk folder. Open the newest recovery link in your browser and choose a new password. Return to the app and sign in.

If a link has expired, request a new one. For a new account, check for a confirmation email before signing in. If messages still do not arrive, contact support with your account email and the approximate request time. Keep the recovery link private.

My data looks different on another device.

Check that you signed in to the same account. Cloud records need an internet connection. Reload before editing on another device: simultaneous edits do not merge. If DebtQuest says your data changed elsewhere, reload and retry the change after checking the latest records.

Device-only records in Safari and the installed iPhone app live separately. Use a backup to move them. The sample journey also has separate storage and does not show your real account.

Your circle

How do invitations work?

Open Circle and choose Start or join a circle → Start a circle for two people or up to eight people total. Share the invitation privately. On the website, open the link, sign in, and join. In the iPhone app, choose Join a circle in that same dialog and paste the invitation link.

A link expires after seven days. Creating another replaces your previous link for that circle. An expired, revoked, or full-circle invitation will not work; ask a member for help. Each account can belong to up to five circles.

Can my partner or friends see my balances?

Circle members see your display name, membership, and the activity you choose to share. A shared payment win includes your overall payoff percentage. It includes the payment amount only when you enable Include payment amounts in Settings and share that payment.

Debt names, starting and remaining balances, APRs, private notes, and personal reward plans are not shared with your circle. Turning a sharing setting off affects future posts. Use Settings → Remove my shared activity to remove earlier posts and reactions in a circle.

How do I revoke a link or leave?

Open Settings and find the circle. Revoke invitation links removes your links; circle owners can revoke all invitations to that circle. This does not remove existing members.

Leave circle removes your membership, activity, and invitations there. If you own it, ownership passes to another member. Your private tracker stays with you.

Keep your circle kind

Encourage progress at each person's pace. Harassment, threats, hate, scams, impersonation, and sharing someone else's private information are not welcome. No one owes a circle their balances or a payment amount.

To report a post, use the menu beside the win. To report a member's name or behavior, tap the member and choose a report reason. Report before blocking if you want us to review activity that blocking will hide.

Blocking hides the other person's name, posts, and reactions from you, and yours from them. It does not remove either person from the circle. Manage blocked members in Settings. Reports are private; operators can review them and remove activity or pause social access when appropriate.

If the activity is no longer available, or you need to explain more, contact us privately. Include the circle name, approximate time, and a description with unrelated personal details removed. For immediate danger, contact your local emergency services; this inbox is not an emergency service.

Records & backups

How do I export or move my progress?

Choose Settings → Export backup and save the JSON file somewhere private, such as Files. On your other device, choose Settings → Import backup and select it.

Import replaces your private tracker after confirmation, so export the current tracker first if you want to keep both. Existing DebtQuest backups are supported. Backups contain private tracker data; they do not restore circle memberships, shared activity, or your login.

Exported files are readable and are not password-protected by DebtQuest. Keep them private. Cloud records appear when you sign in to the same account; local records move only when you explicitly import a backup.

What if a save or import fails?

Check your connection and the error message before leaving the screen. A rejected cloud save does not replace the previous saved records. If another device changed the tracker, reload before retrying.

Choose an original DebtQuest JSON backup for import. An invalid file is rejected without replacing your existing tracker. If the problem persists, contact support with the error message and the steps you took, without attaching your full backup.

What does resetting the tracker do?

Settings → Reset private tracker gives your private debts, payments, progress, and rewards a fresh start. It does not delete your cloud login or circle posts. Remove shared activity separately or use account deletion below to close a cloud account.

Uninstalling the app or clearing browser storage can remove device-only records. Export a backup first if you want to keep them.

Delete your cloud account

  1. Export your private tracker first if you want a copy.
  2. If you own a circle that should continue, choose Settings → Leave circle to pass ownership to another member.
  3. Choose Settings → Delete cloud account.
  4. Enter your current password, type DELETE, and confirm. Keep the app connected until it confirms completion.

This removes your login, private tracker, profile, memberships, posts, reactions, invitations, blocks, and reports you submitted from the active database. Circles you still own are removed for everyone. Other members' private trackers are unaffected.

Reports made by others may remain with references to your deleted account or activity removed. Support correspondence, provider logs, and backups are handled separately as explained in our retention policy. Copies you exported or others saved are not erased.

If you cannot sign in, try password recovery first. For a failed deletion or a request involving support records or other account information, contact us privately. We may need to verify your account ownership. Never send your password.

Payments, points & estimates

Does logging a payment send money?

No. DebtQuest records payments you have already made. Pay your lender through your usual method, then record the payment here. If interest or fees changed the balance, enter the actual new statement balance when logging the payment.

How do points and rewards work?

A daily check-in earns 25 XP. The first logged payment for each payment date earns 50 XP, regardless of amount. Your first encouragement each day earns 15 XP. Repeating an action for the same date does not award extra points.

Spending points on a personal reward does not reduce your lifetime level. Rewards are plans you make for yourself; DebtQuest does not supply products or cash prizes. There is no penalty for missing a day.

Why is my payoff date an estimate?

Projections assume monthly interest, fixed APRs, no new charges, and a constant monthly payment budget that moves to remaining debts as others are paid off. Your lender may calculate interest differently, and fees, payment timing, or rate changes can alter the result.

Use the slider to explore possibilities. DebtQuest is a planning tool and does not provide personalized financial advice; lender statements remain the source for your actual balance and obligations.
